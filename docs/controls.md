# Controls

Back to the [README](../README.md).

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D / arrows | Walk (hold Shift to run); on the ladder, W and S climb |
| Space | Jump (you can land on desks, couches and the cars in the garage) |
| Mouse / A D / W S / Space / Esc (at the golf tee) | The mouse or A and D aim, W and S set the loft; hold Space and let go to hit. Esc, or Leave on the panel, puts the club back (E doesn't); E at the tee again carries on with your round |
| Arrows / W A S D / swipe / Space / P / Esc (at the Snake machine) | **E** at the machine between the TV and the jukebox: the camera glides up to its screen and the first arrow sets the snake off. Arrows, WASD or a swipe steer; Space or P pauses (and carries on); Enter plays again after a game over. A score good enough for the high-score table asks for your name (Enter saves). Esc or **Stop playing** steps away, and what you scored still counts (E doesn't) |
| Mouse drag / wheel | Orbit / zoom the camera |
| E | Interact: hire a worker, open its terminal, read a board, take an issue's note off the board, prompt a board agent, call a meeting in the meeting room, draw on the whiteboard, read the docs at the bookshelf, watch the TV, sit down (or get up), ride the elevator, climb the ladder (or get off it), slide down a fire pole, grab a coffee, take a smoke break, tee off at the golf tee, step up to the dartboard at the oche (or back into your place in its game), step up to the pool table (or back into your seat in its game), play Snake at the Snake machine (or watch whoever's playing it), sit down at the casino's roulette table to bet, pet the dog, pick up the basketball (then hold E and let go to shoot), order a drink at the rooftop bar, blow the DJ's air horn |
| Mouse / Space (at the dartboard) | On your turn, the mouse aims; hold Space and let go when the meter's in the green to throw. Walking off steps away but keeps your place in a running game; Esc or **Leave** on the panel leaves it, and **Skip** passes over someone who's away on their turn |
| Mouse / Space / Q / Esc (at the pool table) | On your shot, move the mouse round the cue ball to aim; press on the table and drag back (or hold Space) and let go to shoot. The dot on the cue ball in the panel sets spin. With ball in hand, drag the cue ball where you want it first. Q goes round to the other side of the table. E at the table does nothing; Esc or walking off steps away, keeping your seat in a running game for you (E there again brings you back, even after a reload). **Leave** in the panel gives it up |
| Mouse / Esc (at the poker table) | The mouse is free for the panel: buy in, then Fold, Check or Call, and Bet or Raise with the slider (½ pot, pot, all-in) on your turn. Walking off stands you up but keeps your seat; Esc or **Leave** gives it up and cashes you out. E at the table does nothing |
| 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / Esc (at the blackjack table) | Sit on one of the casino's blackjack stools to play. On your turn 1 hits, 2 stands, 3 doubles and 4 splits (or use the panel's buttons, which also place your bet and take insurance). E on the stool does nothing; Esc or walking off gets you up, keeping your seat and your hands in a round for you (sit down again to come back). **Leave** in the panel gives it up |
| Space / − + / Esc (at a slot machine) | Space (or **Spin**, or the lever) spins; − and + change the bet. Walking off keeps the machine for you a while (after a reload too); Esc or **Leave** gives it up. E there never gets you up |
| 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / Esc (online blackjack at the PC) | In the boss's chair, **E** and pick **Online Blackjack**. On your turn 1 hits, 2 stands, 3 doubles and 4 splits (or the buttons under the screen, which also bet, take insurance and pull a face). Esc or **Stop playing** steps away, keeping your seat for a few minutes; **Leave** gives it up |
| Click / right-click / Esc (at the roulette table) | Sitting on a roulette stool in the casino: click the layout at the bottom to put a chip of the value you picked down (where in a number you click says the bet: the middle, a line, a corner, the bottom edge), right-click to pick your chips up again, **Clear** and **Rebet** in the panel. E does nothing more; getting up keeps your place and your chips down; Esc or **Leave** leaves the table |
| Click the chip counter (top bar) | Your chips: the balance and your latest changes, with what each was for |
| P | Prompt: give a task to a new worker, or to the one at this desk |
| C | Changes: the files the worker at this desk changed and their diff; commit, discard or open a PR |
| B | Open a shared shell at an empty desk |
| R | Resume a sleeping worker (or restart a shell) |
| X | Send a worker home (frees the desk; a worker with its own worktree asks what to do with it). While your own emote plays, X stops it instead and does nothing else |
| O | Open a pull request for a worker on its own branch, or see the one it has |
| N | Go to the worker that has waited longest on someone; again for the next one |
| F | Hang a picture from the web on a wall (scroll to size it, click to hang it); at the ashtray on the balcony, pick a joint to smoke |
| Q | Put back the issue card you're carrying, or drop the basketball |
| T / Enter | Chat |
| G / 1–6 | Emote: hold G for the wheel (point and let go) or press 1–6 to wave, give a thumbs up, clap, dance, point or facepalm; everyone on your floor sees it |
| L | Blow the whistle, anywhere: hold for a long trill, tap for a short tweet; everyone on your floor hears it |
| / | Search the chat and every terminal on your floor |
| V | Join voice; in voice, hold to talk (you're muted when you let go) |
| M | Mute / unmute in voice |
| Tab | The ☰ menu: every window, and what shows on screen |
| Esc | Close any window (a terminal too) and get back to looking around |
| Ctrl + [ | Send Esc to a terminal (e.g. to interrupt Claude) |
| Drag, then Ctrl + right-click (in a terminal) | Copy text out of a terminal: drag over it to select it, then Ctrl + right-click copies it (no browser menu) and a short **Copied** shows. While a program like Claude uses the mouse, a plain drag goes to the program, so hold **Ctrl** or **Shift** while dragging (**Cmd** or **Option** on a Mac). Lines keep their breaks, without the padding spaces at their ends |
| Ctrl + Shift + C / Ctrl + C (in a terminal) | Copy the selection too (**Cmd + C** on a Mac). Ctrl + C only copies while something is selected (and clears the selection); with nothing selected it's the interrupt, as always. Works over plain HTTP on the LAN as well |

You can also click a nearby desk to interact with it, or click a worker in the Workers panel (**🤖 Workers**, top right) to open its terminal.
